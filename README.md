# BridgeForward

> Bridge the gap between people, media and opportunities, and help them move forward.

BridgeForward is a media-quality and opportunity platform that helps people prepare and share high-quality media on WhatsApp while connecting users, businesses, investors, collaborators and buyers around new opportunities.

## User

**Primary users:**
- Everyday WhatsApp users sharing personal photos/videos and Status updates who want better quality without complicated processes
- Content creators (social/video creators, photographers, influencers/personal brands) who depend on visual quality
- Small businesses and entrepreneurs (online vendors, service providers) using WhatsApp as a major marketing channel

**Secondary users:**
- Businesses/organizations looking for creators or collaborators
- Investors interested in supporting promising products, ideas, and businesses
- Collaborators (designers, marketers, partners) and potential buyers of products/services

## Problem

WhatsApp users experience a noticeable reduction in photo/video quality when sharing or posting, which is frustrating for everyday memories, creator content, and business marketing.

Separately, people building media and digital solutions struggle to find the right investors, collaborators, customers, and buyers.

BridgeForward addresses both through one ecosystem: first solving quality sharing, then bridging people to opportunities.

## Main Journey

Core principle: **Make quality sharing simple, fast and effortless — Select → Prepare → Share.**

1. **Bring media in:** Upload, select from device, or share to BridgeForward from another app
2. **Prepare:** BridgeForward prepares the media for WhatsApp (no technical settings for the user)
3. **Share:** Prominent `Share to WhatsApp`, plus Save to device, Share elsewhere, or Do it later
4. **Reuse:** Access Recent Media history without repeating the process

**Extended journeys (post-MVP):**
- Business owner: prepare media → use on WhatsApp → create business/project profile → discover collaborators/buyers/investors → track activity
- Investor / Collaborator / Buyer: create profile → explore opportunities → express interest / request collaboration → connect → track interactions

## MVP Scope

Per `BRIGDE FORWARD PRD.docx`: onboarding, media selection/upload, simple preparation, WhatsApp sharing, save prepared media, basic history, profile, basic activity tracking. Investor/collaborator/buyer ecosystem starts simple and expands later.
